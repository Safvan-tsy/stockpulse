This is an MS Excel Workbook containing Candle Stick + Delivery Data Charts 
of companies meeting the following 12 fundamental conditions :-

1. PE >0
2. EPS >0
3. Sales Quarter > 0
4. YoY Sales Growth >0
5. Net Profit Quarter >0
6. YoY Net Profit Quarter >-10%
7. Promoter Pledging < 10%
8. Unpledged Promoter Hold > 30%
9. Change in Promo Hold >=0
10. Debt/Eq Ratio Between 0 and 1
11. Current Ratio > 1
12. ROCE >=10

Some
of the companies will have limited data due to change in ISIN/newly 
listed/Series Change etc. This workbook contains BSE+NSE data and not 
auto-updatable as such data doesn't exist in public domain.

Shared for study purpose..... Please use PC to check and learn about the charts.
